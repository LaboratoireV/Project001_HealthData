Project 001

Synthetic Alberta emergency and inpatient data.

Project 001 generates fictional NACRS and DAD records for two questions: whether CTAS and the time before the disposition decision are associated with hospitalization, and, among linked admissions, whether CTAS and emergency stay minutes are associated with inpatient length of stay in days.

Consulting reports

Memo A00 builds the research variables. A01 and A02 describe the analytic files. A03 estimates whether pre-disposition stay and CTAS are associated with hospitalization. A04 links hospitalized emergency visits to inpatient abstracts and compares linked with unlinked visits. A05 estimates whether emergency stay and CTAS are associated with inpatient length of stay in the linked subset. A06 summarizes the design, methods, findings, and limitations.

Responsible by design.

Project 001 uses entirely randomized synthetic records and fictional client labels. The atlas supplies structure and interpretation notes only. It does not host patient-level data or reproduce the official data workbooks.
